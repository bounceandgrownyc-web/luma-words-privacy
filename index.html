Luma Words — Privacy Policy (draft for bounceandgrowstudios.com/luma-words-privacy)

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Luma Words is a word puzzle game developed by Bounce & Grow LLC ("we", "us").

Data we collect

Luma Words does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers are accessed.

    • On-device storage

    Game progress, settings (such as sound on/off), and collected words are saved only on your device so the game can resume where you left off. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

    Internet use

    The app is fully playable offline. It may load font files from Google Fonts when a connection is available; that request is handled by Google's servers under Google's privacy policy and sends no game or personal data from us.

    Children

    Luma Words contains no ads, no chat, no external links for purchases, and no data collection, and is suitable for general audiences.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
